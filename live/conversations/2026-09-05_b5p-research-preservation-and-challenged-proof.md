whole-system inventory, the operator surfaced a different dependency: Ontinuity
already produces behavioral and institutional evidence that cannot be recreated

> "Ontinuity is nothing without the history as recorded in its corpus."

> "Let's make sure we keep it special here too, so it carries forward."

> "Ok. Let's do the steps needed to make that happen. We'll get back on track after that."

The bounded answer became B5-P. It adds provider-neutral evidence preservation
without adding behavioral interpretations, scoring, routing, identity claims,
or a second research ledger. The canonical session database remains the source
of truth.

This followed repeated B0 corrections driven by another operator directive:

> "The answer is always in the corpus."

The inventory had to include the worker institution, Intake versus Design,
Researcher/Challenger gates, Shepherd, Governor, corpus-writing lifecycle,
deployment authority, historical reversals, separately installed box code, and
the behavioral-research substrate—not merely the visible engine files.
