About the builder

Patrick Killebrew

I built Ontinuity to make AI-assisted work more persistent, reviewable, and useful on real problems.

My background spans construction, industrial maintenance, technical installation, and business operations. That experience shapes the questions I bring to a project: How does the work actually happen? Where does it break down? What would make the solution useful to the person who has to use it?

Why Ontinuity

Working with AI made it possible for me to investigate technical questions and build software. It also made the need for continuity and verification concrete. Decisions need to survive a new conversation, assumptions need to remain visible, and a confident answer needs to stand up to review.

Ontinuity is my ongoing work on that problem: persistent project records, defined requirements, adversarial review across multiple models, and a traceable account of what was done.

How I approach a project

I start by understanding the owner’s process and what they need to change. From there, I work toward a clear problem definition, a scoped proposal, and practical stages of implementation and testing.

The home-care project illustrates that approach. Defining the scheduling solution exposed a data-access barrier, so I built SantaClean to help prepare de-identified data for development. The complete scheduler remains a planned next stage.
